## cookiecutter_chartbook

Portions of `templates/project/` are adapted from
[backofficedev/cookiecutter_chartbook](https://github.com/backofficedev/cookiecutter_chartbook):
the command-line / environment / defaults precedence in `src/settings.py`, the
`dodo.py` helpers for executing jupytext notebooks and compiling LaTeX, the
`.github/workflows/tests.yml` setup, and the `\PathToOutput` convention in
`reports/report.tex`. The pull/load and code-style conventions in `SKILL.md`,
`AGENTS.md` and `references/code-style.md` paraphrase that template's
AI-assistant rules.
